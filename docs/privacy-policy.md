# Quran Time — Privacy Policy

*Last updated: 26 November 2026*

Quran Time (وقت القرآن) is a free app for reading the Madinah Mushaf and listening to the Quran.

## What we collect

Nothing. Quran Time does not collect, store or share any personal data.

- There are no accounts and no sign-in.
- There are no ads.
- There is no analytics, crash reporting or tracking of any kind.

## What stays on your phone

Your bookmarks, last page, playback position, favourites, settings and downloaded pages and recitations are stored only on your device. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes them.

## Network access

The app connects only to:

- **mp3quran.net** (its API and the servers it links to) for Mushaf pages, recitations, radio, live TV, videos, tafsir and reflections.

These requests contain only what is needed to fetch the content (for example, the surah number and the language). They do not include anything that identifies you. MP3Quran.net's own privacy practices apply to its servers.

## Permissions

- **Internet:** to load pages, recitations, radio and video.
- **Network state:** to tell you when you're offline, and to wait for a connection before downloading.
- **Notifications (Android 13+):** to show playback controls.
- **Foreground service (media playback) / wake lock:** to keep recitations playing with the screen off.
- **Run at start-up:** so downloads that were still waiting carry on after the phone restarts.

The app asks for no access to your files, photos, contacts, microphone, camera or location.

## Children

The app does not collect data from anyone, including children.
